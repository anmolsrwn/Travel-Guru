How We Keep Costs Low

Operations & transparency

How does an independent agency get net fares?

We work through free B2B consolidator portals that give registered agents access to wholesale airline, hotel, rail and cruise inventory. You get the same rates as large chains without their overheads.

Do you charge subscription or booking fees?

No. Our lean, zero-subscription setup means there are no consultation or booking fees. Our margin is built transparently into your quote.

How much do I pay to confirm a booking?

Packages below ₹25,000 per person: 100% at booking. ₹25,000 and above per person: 50% advance at booking and the balance 21 days before departure. Bookings made within 21 days of departure need 100% payment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Charges per person on the package cost: 46+ days before departure 10%, 31–45 days 35%, 16–30 days 50%, 8–15 days 75%, 0–7 days or no-show 100%. Air tickets, visas, insurance and other non-refundable services follow the supplier's policy. Requests must be sent in writing by email or WhatsApp.

Are GST, TCS and flights included?

Package prices cover hotels, listed meals, transfers and sightseeing. Flights are quoted separately at live fares. GST (currently 5%) and TCS for international tours are extra as per Government of India rules.

What documents do I need?

A passport valid for at least 6 months from the travel date. For e-visas such as Dubai, send a clear scan of the passport and a white-background photo at least 15 days before travel.

Who handles my forex card and cash?

An RBI-authorised forex partner issues your multi-currency card and delivers cash to your door. We coordinate everything for you.
